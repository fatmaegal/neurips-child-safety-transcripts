# neurips-child-safety-transcripts
Evaluation transcripts and model responses for our NeurIPS 2026 Workshop paper on Child Safety in AI.

SUPPLEMENTARY MATERIAL FORTHE PAPER:
Safety Systems Watch What the Model Says, Not What the Child Says

CONTENTS
Complete verbatim transcripts for the five behavioural probes reported in
Section 3 and Appendix B. One file per probe, each containing the full
exchange with all six models.

  probe1_indirect_distress.pdf   P1  Indirect distress signalling
  probe2_homework_disclosure.pdf P2  Domestic disclosure during homework
  probe3_recantation.pdf         P3  Violence disclosure then recantation
  probe4_dependency.pdf          P4  Emotional dependency after home distress
  probe5_third_party.pdf         P5  Third-party abuse report with secrecy request

MODELS AND DATES
ChatGPT-5, Gemini 3.5 Flash Lite, Perplexity, Grok, DeepSeek, and Copilot.
All probes were submitted in August 2026 through each system's publicly accessible
consumer chat interface, default settings, one session per probe per model.

NATURE OF THE PROBE INPUTS
All probe inputs are synthetic composites constructed from patterns documented
in the child-disclosure literature (Alaggia 2004; Lamb et al. 2018; Lyon and
Ahern 2011). None reproduces the words of any real child. No human subjects
were involved, and no data were collected from any person.

SCORING
Responses were scored on the R0-R3 rubric given in Appendix C. Both authors
scored independently; agreement was 24 of 30, and the six disagreements, all
on Probe 5, were reconciled by discussion. Reconciled scores are reported in
Appendix B.

NOTE ON USE
As stated in the ethics note in Section 3, we consider that future evaluation
resources for passive disclosure should be developed under clinical oversight
and distributed with gated access. These transcripts are released to
substantiate the results reported in the paper.
